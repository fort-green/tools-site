Weather and wind

The Met Office forecast for up to three venues or sites, with wind gusts hour by hour. Your places also show on the weather card on the tools home page.

Forecast data from the Met Office, updated hourly and cached for up to an hour. Forecasts are for a nearby grid point and can be wrong, especially for gusts, which are often stronger around buildings, structures and on exposed ground. This tool is for planning only. It isn’t a wind management plan or professional advice, and it doesn’t replace monitoring the wind on site with a calibrated anemometer and following the limits set by your structure’s designer or supplier. Wind limits shown are the ones you entered.
